# Sea of Galilee Boat (The "Jesus Boat")

## What It Is

The **Sea of Galilee Boat** — also called the **Ancient Galilee Boat** or popularly the **"Jesus Boat"** — is a 1st-century C.E. wooden fishing vessel discovered in January 1986 on the northwest shore of the Sea of Galilee, near **Kibbutz Ginosar**. It is now displayed at the **Yigal Allon Center** in Ginosar.

## Why It Matters

- The boat is dated to roughly **the 1st century B.C.E. to 1st century C.E.**, the exact period of Jesus’ ministry on the Sea of Galilee.
- It is the only surviving boat from the time and place of the Gospel narratives about fishermen disciples and storm crossings.
- Its hull is built from **12 different types of wood**, all of which are mentioned in the Bible.

## The Discovery

- Found during a severe drought when the lake level dropped, exposing the muddy lakebed near Ginosar.
- Discovered by brothers Yuval and Moshe Lopian, members of Kibbutz Ginosar.
- Excavated in a complex ten-day operation by the **Israel Antiquities Authority** with many volunteers.
- The boat was preserved in a special pool for 14 years (until 2000) before being placed on public display.

## The Boat

- Length: about **8.2 meters** (27 feet).
- Construction: primarily cedar and oak, with reused timber suggesting a long working life.
- Capable of carrying about 13–15 people.
- Built with both Jewish and Greco-Roman shipbuilding techniques.
- Was likely used for fishing and transport on the lake.

## KJV Connection

> *"And he saw two ships standing by the lake: but the fishermen were gone out of them, and were washing their nets. And he entered into one of the ships, which was Simon's, and prayed him that he would thrust out a little from the land. And he sat down, and taught the people out of the ship."* — Luke 5:2–3 (KJV)

> *"And when even was come, he saith unto them, Let us pass over unto the other side. And when they had sent away the multitude, they took him even as he was in the ship. And there were also with him other little ships."* — Mark 4:35–36 (KJV)

- The Gospels repeatedly describe Jesus teaching from a boat and crossing the Sea of Galilee with his disciples.
- The Ginosar boat gives a concrete example of the kind of vessel used by Galilean fishermen in the first century.
- The area of discovery is close to **Magdala** and the land of **Gennesaret** mentioned in Matthew 14:34.

## Notes

- The nickname "Jesus Boat" is devotional rather than scientific. There is no evidence that Jesus or the apostles used this specific vessel.
- However, its date, location, size, and construction match the boat-travel and fishing scenes described in the Gospels.
- It is one of the most important material-culture finds illustrating the everyday world of the New Testament.
